# Getting data
## From your own property
GA4 → Explore → Free form. Dimensions: Date, Session default channel group. Metrics: Sessions, Total users, New users, Engaged sessions, Key events, Total revenue. Date range: previous + current month. Share/Export → Download CSV.

## Practising with Google's public demo account
Google offers a public GA4 demo account (Google Merchandise Store) that anyone with a Google account can add: search "Google Analytics demo account" in Google's Analytics Help. Export as above. This pack ships NO numbers from the demo account; use your own export.
